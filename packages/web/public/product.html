Fund the terms

The provider funds a fixed amount. The customer accepts the endpoint, oracle roster and outage threshold before monitoring begins.

Require agreement

The same quorum must corroborate the full outage streak. Each oracle signs its own report and anchors the evidence to the contract.

Verify the settlement

After a breach the customer can claim the escrow. The finalized transfer and callback provide a record both parties can verify.
